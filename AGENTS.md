# Portfolio repository guidance

## Purpose

This public repository contains the website source and deployable configuration for Dan Hughes's portfolio. The private career sourcebook is a separate evidence system and must never be copied or linked into this repository.

## Public-content boundary

- Use British English throughout visible copy, titles, metadata and documentation. Do not use em dashes. Keep required code/API spellings unchanged.

- Use **Observability, SRE & AI Leader** as the prominent homepage headline, with Dan's name secondary. Derive the browser title from the same content value. It is positioning, not a formal employment title; AI refers to hands-on tooling and team engineering practices, not model research or enterprise AI strategy.
- Keep that h1 static. The terminal subtitle belongs immediately beneath it, with source-reviewed phrases, repeated hold/delete/type cycles and a blinking caret. Suspend typing and blinking offscreen, when hidden and for reduced motion; reserve all phrase sizes to prevent layout shifts. No separate subtitle pause control, per the owner's request. This is a recorded accessibility compromise, not grounds for a blanket WCAG conformance claim.
- Shuffle through the complete subtitle phrase set before reuse, avoiding adjacent repeats across set boundaries. Keep phrases distinct rather than repeating the same rollout wording. Check motion gates before a queued callback changes text, not only after a preference event.
- Keep SRE and infrastructure primary; AI is supporting evidence rather than a claim of AI/ML security expertise.
- Do not publish employer names, formal employment titles, employment dates or career chronology in copy, metadata, structured data, images, source maps or downloads.
- Do not publish a claim until its exact wording has an approved public-use decision in the private sourcebook.
- Never infer personal ownership, adoption, scale or outcome from platform access or team activity.
- The recorded profiling publication check distinguishes authentication failures from transient upstream errors. Do not describe it as an unconditional guarantee that live rules precede every publication; the private source account records a fail-open exception.
- Use a personal homepage, `/notes/` for engineering depth and `/experiments/` for optional experiments. Main navigation labels are single words: Notes, Experiments, Contact. Do not impose a single-page layout, three evidence entries or an application-form structure.
- Lead with concrete engineering decisions and personality, not abstract business slogans. Personal interests must be sourced; never invent hobbies or favourites.
- Browser-local simulations must be clearly synthetic, keyboard usable and optional. The Radar atlas is separately labelled observed data. Never fabricate production telemetry, terminal output or operational status.
- The Internet atlas automatically requests Radar on initial load and refresh. Its Traffic, Bots, Devices and Protocols tabs share country selection and source metadata. Missing readings are gaps; reported disruptions must not be inferred from traffic dips. Failures clear readings without substituting samples. Initial and no-script views show an empty chart, never a sample that flashes before live data. Reserve interactive geometry before module initialisation. Keep the Radar token server-side and retain the owner's recorded licence decision, attribution and reuse notices.
- The Internet atlas sits directly on the page without a coloured panel. Keep accent colour on selected markers and the traffic line, not a large map backdrop.
- Separate development and production Radar Read credentials are stored in 1Password; retrieve references from the private operational record. Expiry and token IDs belong in the vault, not this repository. Deliver only to Access-protected, non-indexable `dev.danhughes.uk`. Production at `danhughes.uk` is on hold until fresh owner approval. Keep `PRODUCTION_DEPLOY_ENABLED` absent or false. Verify actual deployment state rather than inferring it from configuration.
- Radar reading guides sit below the shared map/chart surface, with an accessible description association. Keep chart alignment and mobile reflow in the geometry tests. The selected-country pulse loops gently while visible, suspends offscreen/hidden and respects reduced motion. There is no page-level background-motion button, per the owner's request. It is a selection cue, not a ping or measured activity.
- Radar loads automatically on opening, reload and country/view selection. Show only source update metadata in normal operation, without a manual refresh icon, spinner, completion message or client fetch timestamp. Offer Retry Radar only after a failed request; successful retry hides it again.
- Preserve the document-memory Radar cache and bounded speculative queue in `src/experiments/radar-cache.ts`. Reuse validated responses only within its window; do not describe that as upstream freshness. Keep foreground priority, identical-request coalescing, exploration/visibility/connection-hint gating and document-level speculative backoff. Loading/unavailable placeholders are distinct from N/A null readings; source timestamps and Worker limits remain authoritative.
- Omit absent Radar confidence from visible metadata. Preserve null in the data contract, and continue showing supplied confidence ratings and source-quality warnings in every view.
- Motion is progressive enhancement: respect reduced motion, keep content visible without scripts and avoid scroll hijacking.
- Keep initial loads, refreshes and restored section links settled. Homepage note-preview links have no entrance reveal; animate only their artwork on deliberate hover/focus. Other offscreen reveals must not affect content already in view. Preload local fonts and avoid late font swaps that shift readable content. Use authored SVGs for prominent arrows rather than emoji-prone text glyphs. Include iPhone 17 Pro-sized WebKit checks without claiming physical Safari testing.
- The Notes page lives at `/notes/`; titles and destination labels use Notes, not Engineering notes. Do not retain `/work/` or `/playground/` forwarding pages: the site is pre-publication and all links must use current routes. The homepage `#work` anchor remains separate.
- Note anchors describe the overall practices: `observability`, `ai-tooling`, `kubernetes-iac` and `technical-leadership`. Homepage previews, Notes navigation, article IDs and diagram styles must agree. Do not retain legacy aliases before publication.
- Kubernetes and infrastructure as code must be visible in the opening and featured work, substantiated by the recorded configuration, Helm delivery, Terraform and rollout decisions. Do not turn this into unsupported cluster ownership or a keyword inventory.
- Keep the homepage connection graphic distinct from Experiments. It is abstract, browser-local geometry, not telemetry. It pulses continuously from different nodes while visible, with one animation-frame chain and no adjacent automatic origin repeat. Stop offscreen/hidden, on page suspension and for reduced motion; resume only when active. The waveform lives only on Experiments and adds synthetic interference, not signal recovery or incident detection. The dh signature trace redraws on hover/focus; its endpoint appears only when the stroke finishes. Reduced motion shows the complete static mark.
- The dh signature draw completes after pointer or keyboard focus leaves. Re-entry must not restart an active stroke; show its endpoint only at completion. Reduced-motion changes cancel to the complete static mark. The favicon is the plain dh monogram, without an accent or underline.
- Keep private evidence labels, internal URLs, identifiable incidents and employer-derived assets out of Git and generated output.

## Secrets

- Never commit or print API tokens, service credentials, Turnstile secret keys, private contact routing or values from `.env` and `.dev.vars` files.
- Keep `.env*`, `.dev.vars*`, Wrangler local state and generated output ignored. An `.env.example` may contain key names and safe descriptions only.
- Use environment-scoped GitHub secrets or Cloudflare secret bindings for deployment and runtime credentials.
- A variable exposed to Astro client code is public. Do not put a secret in a public-prefixed variable.
- Pull-request workflows must not receive production credentials.
- If a credential is committed, rotate it immediately; deleting it from the latest revision is not remediation.

## Engineering expectations

- Review content, visual hierarchy and interaction as one unit. Every featured project must identify Dan's supported contribution, a specific decision and its qualitative consequence; a tool list or anonymous project description is insufficient.
- Explanatory diagrams must explain the adjacent engineering decision. Label conceptual demonstrations and never imply that synthetic traces are production evidence. The decorative homepage connection field is a separate abstract accent, not a career-evidence diagram.
- Note-preview links share a consistent hover and keyboard-focus response, with restrained motion inside the graphic and static captions. Respect reduced motion, avoid sticky touch hover and keep first-tap navigation working. Non-interactive diagrams within notes should not mimic clickable previews.
- Light and dark palettes are both designed states. Support system preference, an explicit persistent override, reduced motion, keyboard controls and no-JavaScript fallback; test every primary route in both palettes.
- Use an icon button for theme selection, not a dropdown. Its icon and accessible label must describe the selected preference, including system mode; state the next action in the label.
- External Hello/Contact links open in a new tab with an accessible new-tab description and `rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Internal Contact navigation remains in the current tab.
- The Experiments "Explore the source" link follows the same protected new-tab convention.
- The shared footer's View source link also opens a protected new tab and describes that behaviour accessibly.
- The shared footer reads "Always tinkering." Notes and Experiments use the same `page-intro` heading treatment, with natural wrapping rather than forced heading breaks. Notes has no Back home links; retain home navigation through the shared mark and footer identity, and keep its closing Connect action.
- The shared `page-intro` owns the divider below Notes and Experiments introductions. Do not add a second border to Notes content. Both content areas begin with the shared section spacing.
- Typography uses the shared body, display and monospace families, weight tokens, and body/reading/small/caption/micro size roles in `global.css`. Do not reintroduce near-identical one-off UI text sizes or an undefined body-font token. Fluid editorial headings, the terminal subtitle and diagram-space text retain purposeful sizing; the terminal heading uses monospace, not the display face.
- Keep the theme control visually unframed and subordinate to navigation, with a small icon inside a full 44 px touch target.
- Accessibility includes keyboard behaviour and assistive-technology semantics, not only an automated score. Verify skip-link focus, visual/DOM order, landmarks, heading hierarchy, meaningful control values and concise link names. Prefer native elements; add ARIA only for information they do not already expose. Do not announce animation frames to screen readers or claim VoiceOver/NVDA testing without actually running it.
- Functional tests are necessary, not a design verdict. Inspect complete sections visually and ask whether the work distinguishes Dan without generic superiority claims or unsupported ownership.
- Reflow checks must cover actual section containment, inter-section gaps and numeric labels, not only document overflow. Do not use percentage row gaps in intrinsic-height stacked sections; keep index numbers unbroken.
- Browser tests must account for fractional DOMRect rounding without tolerating visible overlap. Do not assume wheel input gives an exact scroll distance or that a fresh media query proves existing preference listeners have updated. Assert the relevant layout, focus and motion state across every configured engine.
- Use the shared spacing and responsive heading tokens in `src/styles/global.css` for route rhythm and proportionate typography. Keep readable body copy, illustration aspect ratios, stable experiment frames and touch-target minimums as purposeful exceptions. The decorative homepage connection field keeps one authored asymmetric arrangement across reloads; preserve its fixed frame, no-JavaScript fallback and reduced-motion presentation. Randomise pulse origins, not node positions. Do not restore random layout selection or the network's heartbeat glyph, or confuse it with the separate dh signature mark.
- Keep the homepage connection graphic a compact accent. Note-preview artwork shares a compact size scale, including the leadership typography; allow enlarged text to grow rather than clip. The faint background grid follows both palettes across every route and disappears in forced colours or increased contrast.
- The connection graphic belongs beside the introduction on desktop and below it on mobile, not beside the positioning headline. Keep its caption aligned with the compact, wide drawing. The subtitle stays directly below the h1, with only a small margin above the divider.
- Inactive Radar views and reading alternatives retain intrinsic responsive space while remaining hidden and inert. Radar has one footer, with description and tab-specific context grouped on the left, and reading guidance with the persistent data/licence credit on the right. Do not split these into separate bordered rows. Stack the columns in reading order at the map/chart breakpoint. Keep long data readable and preserve the user's event-disclosure state during ordinary repaint; do not duplicate source links.
- Experiment graphics and their Explore targets keep the same dimensions and position when opened or closed. Place controls below the disclosure, outside the visual frame; retain readable, unclipped content with enlarged text. Test repeated clicks at the original pointer position.
- Live Cloudflare experiments reuse `ExperimentCard.astro` and the same staggered collection, numbered headings, fixed frames and Explore disclosure as the simulations. Do not create a second visual system. Keep observed data and real inference distinct from illustrative previews, and never render fictitious health history to fill a frame.
- All experiment cards use the shared peach, sage and lilac tone tokens. Mix neutral surfaces with selected tinted panels; avoid repeated tinted backgrounds in adjacent cards or down a desktop column. Carry the accent through the artwork rather than filling every panel. Live previews respect visibility and reduced motion. The edge illustration keeps moving when Explore is open. The health preview illustrates check cadence, then shows observed history on opening; room preview motion is illustrative and stops when opened, with received events animated separately.
- After changing shared component markup or scripts, verify the actual development URL as well as the production build. If the preview serves a stale transform, restart only that project's dev server and retest.
- A real received room pulse travels across the complete window A / shared sequence / window B diagram, with a brief centre dwell and receiver highlight. Cancel both travel and arrival effects on disconnect or reduced motion. This illustrates a broadcast; it is not a measured network route or latency.
- Joining the room receives a typed current-state snapshot, not a new pulse. Clear the sequence on disconnect. Gate event animation on the diagram's visibility separately from the card's connection lifetime. Keep health windows explicitly dated and do not join chart lines across failed or missing slots. Use `docs/experiments.md` as the cross-experiment review contract.
- A visible Connect action must not depend on a cached intersection notification. Check current card bounds; observers still disconnect an off-screen card. Health interval, window and expected-count definitions live in `src/experiments/health-model.ts`, shared by the server and renderer. Reject unordered, duplicated or out-of-window rows before drawing.
- Keep world labels inside the canvas and separate from neighbouring labels and projected nodes. Draw labels after nodes, with stable placement priority and short connectors when displaced. Room pulse travel must not obscure its centre label. Check actual rendered bounds, not only animation keyframes.

- Essential content must be statically rendered and usable without client JavaScript.
- Use semantic HTML, modern standards-based CSS and progressive enhancement. Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
- Keep dependencies proportionate to the feature. The design direction is a personal engineering playground: distinctive typography, purposeful interaction and an obvious path to substantive work.
- Experiments quantitative models are deterministic, browser-local and synthetic. Show previews immediately in a staggered gallery with native DOM order and a single-column mobile layout. Background previews suspend offscreen/hidden, while opened and for reduced motion, without a page-level pause button. Load optional renderers on opening; stop their animation while closed/offscreen/hidden and retain per-experiment pause, replay/reset and native keyboard controls. Logical results and approval controls must not wait for an animation to finish. Keep readable alternatives and do not equate a visual toy with career evidence. Automatic background motion without a user pause mechanism is a recorded accessibility compromise, not blanket WCAG conformance.
- The Kubernetes experiment is a CPU-only HPA snapshot and illustrative placement model, not a full scheduler or VPA implementation. The agent/MCP experiment remains scripted, with host-owned approval policy and a tool-call budget. Separately approved edge experiments use bounded Worker services: allowlisted request metadata, scheduled health samples, fixed-scenario AI inference and one shared coordination room. Never connect a real cluster, send employer data, accept arbitrary AI prompts or enable paid fallback. Follow `docs/architecture.md` and the implementation's limits.
- Treat `wrangler.jsonc` as the source of truth for Cloudflare Worker configuration.
- Keep logs and traces explicitly enabled in its shared observability block, with independent sampling and query-string redaction. Compare dashboard snippets before copying them; do not accidentally disable traces or increase sampling. Verify deployed telemetry separately from local configuration, following `docs/operations.md`.
- Keep development and production Workers, configuration and credentials separate.
- Run formatting, linting, type checking, tests, accessibility checks and the production build before merging.
- `npm run quality` is the local/CI gate: formatting, focused lint, types, model/Worker/static/environment/runtime tests, a safe development build and all configured browsers. Local `PORTFOLIO_BROWSERS=chromium,webkit` selection is explicit; CI runs every engine and rejects overrides. Report the known local Firefox launch gap rather than treating it as a pass.
- Build defaults are development and non-indexable. Production requires `SITE_ENV=production` and a validated HTTPS `SITE_URL` origin. Keep canonical, OG, structured metadata and sitemap production-only. Metadata uses existing public identity/links, with no employer chronology or unsupported claims.
- Preserve Astro's client-environment optional-import module-preload hook and page-reload recovery. The generated `_headers` hashes actual inline script/style bytes; only style attributes allow inline CSS. Worker API responses use the shared security policy. Playwright serves generated assets through the fake-data workerd harness, not a policy-free static server.
- Keep native ingress/cold rate bindings separate by environment. Constant keys avoid visitor identifiers; limits are coarse, eventually consistent and per location. Never claim a global quota or share pending I/O promises across requests. Non-429 failures back off per origin, cache successes remain usable, and protection/cache failures must fail closed.
- Automated runtime tests use only an explicitly fake credential and intercepted upstream fixtures. Never read real credentials into tests. Regenerate Worker binding types from the config after changes. Worker dry-run output belongs in dated scratch, not the checkout.
- The quality job is read-only, SHA-pinned and has no deployment secrets. The dependent deployment job accepts trusted `develop` pushes; production additionally requires the explicit enable variable after fresh owner approval. Use branch-restricted GitHub environments. Apply additive D1 migrations before uploading the checked Worker. Stream runtime secrets through a real OS pipe, not argv or checkout files. Development requires hostname-based Access, which supports the WebSocket experiment; disable workers.dev and preview URLs to prevent alternate routes. The owner has chosen to use Radar under CC BY-NC 4.0; preserve attribution and licence/transform notices without implying vendor confirmation. Never enable a paid plan or rely on a per-location rate limiter as a global AI quota.
- Access-fronted development uses the bounded D1 Radar cache, not Cache API. Scheduled health checks measure the same environment through ASSETS, not the held production site or the external network. Run health and Radar collection independently, report failures without secrets, and retain honest measurement labels. Detailed architecture and operations belong in `docs/`; keep README concise.
- Local Worker development is loopback-only and disables remote bindings. The local command injects `LOCAL_PREVIEW:true` because Wrangler rewrites request origins; all deployed configuration keeps it false. This flag controls local presentation, not authorisation. Build assets and apply local migrations first. `npm run dev:sample` runs the real local scheduled handler once; local Cron is not automatic. Never backfill fabricated health readings. Suppress Wrangler's placeholder edge metadata and explain that Workers AI inference needs the deployed service; reject local inference before reserving budget. A scenario change must leave the new selection ready, not overwrite it with the old request's cancellation message.
- Inspect `dist/` recursively before deployment for secrets, private evidence and environment mistakes.
- Verify deployed behaviour through the actual URL, headers, metadata and page content; a successful upload alone is not completion.

## Git workflow

- Keep the public README concise: identity, stack, local commands, checks and honest delivery state. Detailed operational history and QA records do not belong in the introduction.

- Feature branches target `develop`.
- `develop` is the persistent development environment.
- Production promotion uses a pull request from `develop` to `main`.
- Do not push directly to protected branches, force-push or bypass required checks.
- The required `quality` job always reports a result. `scripts/ci-changes.mjs` skips expensive steps for recognised documentation-only changes; unknown paths, site content, assets, tests and configuration require the full gate. Development deployment requires the job's `code` output. Do not use workflow-level PR path filters that leave required checks pending. Failed change detection must fail the required job.
- Documentation-only runs retain redacted secret scanning. Workflow concurrency uses `queue: max` with cancellation disabled, including PR runs, so a later docs-only push cannot discard a pending code deployment. The pinned actionlint lacks this field; retain only the exact diagnostic exception in `.github/actionlint.yaml` until upstream support lands. Verify the final deployed SHA after a burst of pushes; queue order is not commit order.
- Keep deployment workflows least-privileged and pin third-party actions to reviewed commit SHAs.
